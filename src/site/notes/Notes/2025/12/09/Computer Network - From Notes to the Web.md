## Why This Stack? Comparing Solutions

There are many ways to build a website today. Here's a quick comparison of common alternatives and why the **Obsidian + Github + Vercel + Cloudflare** combination is superior for a personal digital garden.

| Solution                              | Pros                                          | Cons                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WordPress/Wix/Squarespace             | Easy to start, no technical knowledge needed. | Data Lock-in: Your content is trapped in their database. Hard to migrate. Not suited for a networked "garden" of notes. |
| Static Site Generators (Jekyll, Hogo) | Fast websites, you own the data.              | **High Friction**: Requires using the command line and managing complex build environments on your computer.            |
| Notion/Evernote Publish               | Very easy to publish existing notes.          | **Limited Customization & Control**: You are stuck with their look and feel. You don't truly own the final website.     |
